SUBMIT

Run the checklist, then turn in your finished piece by pasting its Google Drive link into the submission form — no more uploading the whole video.

DeadlineRead this

Pieces are due before class starts on your air day. Not done by the deadline = an automatic 0 for the week — no exceptions, because the show airs whether your piece is ready or not. Submit early; a rendered file beats a perfect idea that misses the bell.

Pre-Submit Checklist

Self-check before you turn in — these mirror the automatic deductions, so catch the point-losers now.

Get Your Drive Link

We turn in a link, not the file — it keeps our Drive from filling up. Takes 30 seconds:

  1. Upload your exported video to your own Google Drive (your school account).
  2. Right-click the file → Share.
  3. Under “General access,” switch Restricted → “Anyone with the link.” Leave the role as Viewer.
  4. Click Copy link.
  5. Paste that link into the submission form below.

If a leader can’t open your link, it isn’t shared — re-check step 3. YouTube (unlisted) links work too.

Turn It In

Checklist done and link copied? Open the submission form, fill in the details (name, period, group, piece type, title), and paste your Drive link — you don’t upload the video itself.

The link lands on the producer’s desk and plays right inside the gradebook when it’s graded.
